Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

"Assia Job Automation" is a personal tool used by a single person, Assia Elcadi, with her own Google account.

Data accessed

  • Google Docs and Google Drive: read her resume and create adapted copies of it in her own Drive.
  • Google Sheets: read and add rows in her own job-tracking spreadsheet.
  • Gmail: send a summary email to herself. It does not read her emails.

Storage and sharing

Data stays in her own Google account and on her own computer. It is not sold or shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising. Job offer descriptions are sent to Google Gemini only to compare them with her resume.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
