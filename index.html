Assia Job Automation

A personal automation tool used only by Assia Elcadi, running on her own computer (n8n).

It reads public job offers, keeps the ones that match her profile, prepares a copy of her resume in her own Google Drive, logs the offers in her own Google Sheet and sends her a daily summary email from her own Gmail account.

It is not a public service: no other user can sign in, and it never applies to jobs or contacts anyone on her behalf.
